Overview

Putting You On brings together two things I love: visuals and music. I put people on to songs they might not have heard of, paired with a visual that I feel further represents the song. I make all of the visuals myself.

How I make them

I pick a song I think more people should hear, then shoot and edit a visual for it: filmed on my own camera and edited in DaVinci Resolve.
