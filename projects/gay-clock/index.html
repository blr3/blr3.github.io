Gay Clock

Overview

A satirical “time converter” poking fun at the stereotype that queer people move faster in relationships. I built it to find out, with a survey, some math, and a lot of jokes.

Method

  1. Survey: 24 questions in Google Forms, grouped into four tiers: identity, talking stage, dating, and relationships.
  2. Normalize: each answer is converted to days or a count, then scaled to 0–100, so a higher score always means faster.
  3. Custom scales for the subjective questions (“Speed Runner” vs. “Slow Burner”).
  4. Weight each question, apply age and zodiac multipliers (for fun), and average the scores by how people identify.

Result

Plot twist: straight respondents moved 15.6% faster than queer respondents. The data undercut the joke.

What I learned

  • The questions were too general to separate the groups clearly.
  • Open-ended “Other” answers were hard to quantify, so round two drops them.
  • There wasn't enough data for a reliable correlation, so I set the weights by judgment and cultural references.
